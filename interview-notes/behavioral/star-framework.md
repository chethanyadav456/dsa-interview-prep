# STAR Framework

- **Situation**: set the context in 1-2 sentences.
- **Task**: what was my role or responsibility.
- **Action**: the specific steps I took. This is ~70% of the answer. Use "I", not "we".
- **Result**: the outcome, with a metric where possible, plus what I learned.

## Rules

- 2-3 minutes per answer. Practice out loud with a timer.
- Prepare 6-7 stories that each cover several principles, not one story per question.
- Store stories as bullets, not scripts.
- Every story needs a number (latency, %, users, hours saved, money).
- Failure stories must end with what changed afterward.

## Pitfalls

- Saying "we" throughout, so the interviewer cannot tell what I did.
- No result, or a result with no number.
- Blaming others in conflict stories.
- Failure story with no lesson.
- Rambling setup, thin action.

## Follow-ups to prepare

- What would you do differently?
- What was your specific role?
- How did you measure success?
- What did the other person think?
