# Google (Googliness Interview)

Based on Google's hiring values. Show collaboration, humility, comfort with ambiguity.

Source for questions and "How to answer": the [article](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/behavioral-interview-preparation). "Sample outline" lines are illustrative STAR shapes, not my real stories. Replace them with my own and fill the Story # field.

## 1. Tell me about a time you worked with someone very different from you.

- **Sample outline**: S: paired with a designer who thought in visuals, I thought in code. T: ship a feature together. A: learned their vocabulary, used prototypes instead of specs. R: shipped a week early, now we co-plan every sprint.
