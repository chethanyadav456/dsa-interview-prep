# Amazon (Bar Raiser Round)

Based on Amazon's Leadership Principles. Every answer needs a metric and "I".

Source for questions and "How to answer": the [article](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/behavioral-interview-preparation). "Sample outline" lines are illustrative STAR shapes, not my real stories. Replace them with my own and fill the Story # field.

## 1. Tell me about a time you took ownership of a project.
- **How to answer**: proactively took responsibility and drove results.
- **Sample outline**: S: release pipeline had no owner and failed weekly. T: nobody was assigned, I volunteered. A: audited failures, fixed the top 3 flaky steps, wrote a runbook. R: failures down from 5/week to 0-1, deploy time cut 40%.
