# Meta (Execution & Leadership Interviews)

Show speed, impact, and data-backed influence.

Source for questions and "How to answer": the [article](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/behavioral-interview-preparation). "Sample outline" lines are illustrative STAR shapes, not my real stories. Replace them with my own and fill the Story # field.

- **Sample outline**: S: users needed export. T: own it from design to launch. A: wrote the spec, built backend and UI, tested with 5 users, shipped behind a flag. R: 30% of active users used it in a month.
