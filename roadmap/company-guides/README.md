# Company Guides (5-week plans)

Company-specific interview prep from [Optimizing your interviews at FAANG](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/optimizing-your-interviews-at-faang). Each guide has the company's question patterns, a resource list and a 5-week plan: weeks 1-4 are problems plus study actions, week 5 is timed mock interviews and a feedback loop.

## How to use

1. Pick the company, read its focus and resources, then follow the 5 weeks in order.
2. Hit the time targets in each week's goals; if you go over, review and re-solve later.
3. Do the study actions (hand-write code, draw trees and DP tables, explain aloud).
4. Week 5: three timed 45-minute mocks. Redo anything that took too long. Record yourself explaining.
5. These guides also fit Days 56-57 of the [60-day plan](../60-day-plan.md), the company simulation days.

Problem tags show where the same problem already appears: curated company lists ([companies.md](../companies.md)), the 60-day plan and the 10-week plan.
